# Phishing Email Investigation

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the investigation and analysis of a suspicious email using a simulated Security Operations Centre (SOC) environment on LetsDefend.

The objective was to identify potential phishing indicators, examine email authentication mechanisms, investigate suspicious URLs, and determine whether the email posed a security threat.

The investigation provided practical exposure to common SOC analyst responsibilities, particularly email threat analysis, threat intelligence, and identifying Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).

## Objectives

- Investigate suspicious email characteristics.
- Analyze sender information and email headers.
- Examine SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication results.
- Investigate suspicious URLs.
- Use threat intelligence platforms to assess potential threats.
- Identify Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).
- Determine the appropriate security verdict.

## Tools and Platforms

- LetsDefend
- VirusTotal
- URLScan
- Email Header Analysis Tools

## Skills Demonstrated
